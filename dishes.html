Top Traditional Dishes

  • Biryani: Spiced rice with meat and saffron — a national favorite!
  • Nihari: Slow-cooked beef stew often served for breakfast.
  • Karahi: Chicken or mutton cooked in tomatoes and spices.
  • Chapli Kebab: Minced meat patty from Peshawar.
  • Sajji: Balochi dish of roasted whole lamb or chicken.
